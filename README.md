# PlayerStatus-Game-Engine
PlayerStatus Game Engine – Interactive Player Management and Testing Framework

**PlayerStatus Game Engine** is a sophisticated and interactive framework that empowers developers to simulate various aspects of player management, artifact handling, weapon equipping, movement control, and strategic combat in a gaming environment. The implementation includes dynamic functionalities and an extensive testing module for robust gameplay mechanics.

_**Key Features:**_

-Comprehensive Player Management: Initialize players with customized attributes such as nickname, health, score, and lives.

-Artifact Interaction System: Utilize mathematical evaluations (perfect numbers, prime numbers, etc.) to dynamically affect player stats—boost scores, recover health, gain lives, or encounter penalties.

-Weapons System: Equip players with weapons (knife, sniper, or Kalashnikov) based on their score thresholds, integrating strategic choices into gameplay.

-Movement and Positioning: Enable players to navigate the game world, update positions, and compute distances to strategically engage opponents.

-Combat Mechanics: Assess battle outcomes based on weapon strength, distance, and player attributes, encouraging strategic decision-making during gameplay.

-Game Configuration: Dynamically set game name and adapt player interactions based on game scenarios.

_**Testing Features:**_

-Sanity Checks: Validate functionality with basic input-output tests.

-Field and Method Testing: Evaluate getters, setters, and initializers for seamless functionality and integration.

-Artifact Testing: Simulate artifact discovery scenarios to ensure accurate handling of traps, score adjustments, health changes, and gaining lives.

-Weapon Acquisition Testing: Test the spending of scores on weapons and validate weapon equipping logic based on thresholds.

-Combat Scenarios: Engage players in close-distance duels and evaluate outcomes based on weapon comparison and player stats.

-Distance-Based Combat: Simulate long-distance battles to test weapon superiority logic and adaptability.

_**Benefits:**_

-Provides an interactive testing environment for game development, ensuring smooth integration and functionality.

-Enables detailed simulation of player interactions, artifact effects, and combat scenarios.

-Supports the creation of immersive gaming experiences with strategic mechanics and flexible configurations.
